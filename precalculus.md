These notes were written for students who have finished college algebra and are
heading toward calculus, linear algebra, or a first course in data science.
Every chapter is one web page with interactive figures; nothing needs to be
installed.

**How to read them.** Chapter 0 is reference material — skim it now and come
back when the algebra bites. After that the chapters are meant to be read in
order, since each one leans on the last.
