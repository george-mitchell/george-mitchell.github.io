Welcome to my Homepage!

I am a Doctoral Lecturer in the department of Mathematics and Computer Science at John Jay College of Criminal Justice.

My current research interests are in applied statistics, causal inference and machine learning.

I obtained a PhD in Mathematics from the City University of New York, in the field of Arithmetic Geometry. I completed an MMath at Newcastle University in the UK.

After obtaining my PhD, I gained industry experience in data science and statistical analysis. I am a graduate of the Erdos Institute. You can see some of my projects on my github page.
